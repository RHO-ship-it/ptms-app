Proficiency Testing: Improving Laboratory Performance and Client Confidence

Proficiency testing is a way to ensure the validity of routine laboratory test results by comparing them to pre-established criteria within the framework of inter-laboratory comparisons.

Advantages of proficiency testing
  • Performance evaluation: an analysis of the accuracy and reliability of your results.
  • Strengthening customer trust: a guarantee of the quality of services provided.
  • Problem identification: a detection of potential gaps in the laboratory processes.
Services offered by TTS PT

TTS PT organizes proficiency testing campaigns in the textiles/apparel and leather sectors, offering programs tailored to various methods to meet the diverse needs of participants. Our accreditation body EGAC is a signatory to the ILAC Mutual Recognition Arrangement (ILAC MRA), ensuring global recognition of our proficiency testing programs. For complete information on the organization of these tests, please consult our TTS PT protocol.
